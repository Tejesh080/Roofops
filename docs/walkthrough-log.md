journey (`docs/manual-acceptance-script.md`) and adds one line below: the date, the path, and what broke or "nothing".
A finding here outranks anything in the test catalogue (master plan §13 rule 4).

from the projects' Automation history and the Automation page. More than three on one field: that field becomes
dashboard-only within the following week (the retreat trigger, `docs/editing-surfaces.md`). Note the count in the line.
